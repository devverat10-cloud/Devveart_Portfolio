Design Strategy & Philosophy

Why These Decisions Matter

A breakdown of the visual direction, behavioral psychology, and foundational assumptions driving AstroGuide.

01

Visual Direction & Why I Chose It

Instead of using the clichéd dark-purple and mystical star theme, I designed a "Warm Vedic Trust" aesthetic. The palette pairs warm amber/saffron (#E65100) with a calming ivory background (#FAF8F5) and deep charcoal typography (Plus Jakarta Sans).

Rationale: Our target users (25–45+ years from metro to tier-2/3 towns) seek guidance during anxious life moments (marriage, career, family). A bright, warm, and grounded palette evokes reassurance, hope, and authenticity rather than superstition. Clean spacing and large touch targets (52px buttons) ensure effortless usability for users with lower digital literacy.
02

One Design Decision I Am Proud Of

Contextualizing the 100 Coins on the Gift Screen:
Rather than simply displaying "You received 100 coins", I broke down their exact purchasing power into 3 clear cards (Chat at 10 coins/msg, Audio Consultation, and Kundali Session).

Impact: For first-time and non-tech-savvy users, virtual coins feel abstract. Grounding the coins into concrete actions reduces confusion and immediately motivates exploration on the homepage.
03

Assumptions Made

Three foundational behavioral hypotheses shaped the low-friction user architecture:

• Passwordless OTP Login: Assumed OTP over phone number is the most frictionless login method for tier-2/3 users.
• Coin Economy: Assumed standard chats cost ~10–15 coins/message, meaning 100 welcome coins allow users to test 1–2 real queries for free.
• Live Availability: Assumed astrologers have real-time status indicators (Green dot = Online & ready to chat immediately).
